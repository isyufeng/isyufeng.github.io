About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Queen Mary, University of London, working on natural language processing with a focus on claim detection, evidence retrieval, and explainable fact-checking for online misinformation. Before starting my PhD, I spent 3 years in industry as a software engineer, building ML platforms, payment systems, and leading back-end teams at production scale. I am comfortable across the full stack — from formulating research questions and training models to shipping them as reliable services. I am looking for research roles where both halves matter.
